## 2. The Four-Stage Evasion Playbook
1. **AIS Manipulation (Spoofing & "Going Dark"):**
   Vessels disable their mandatory Automatic Identification System (AIS) transponders or use GPS spoofing software to transmit fake coordinates (e.g., broadcasting coordinates showing the ship anchored off the UAE while physically loading crude in Kharg Island, Iran or Primorsk, Russia).
2. **Ship-to-Ship (STS) Transfers in International Waters:**
   Crude is loaded onto a "feeder" tanker at a sanctioned port, sailed into international waters, and pumped directly via flexible hoses onto a larger Very Large Crude Carrier (VLCC). Key STS transfer hubs:
   - The **Laconian Gulf** off the southern coast of Greece (outside the 6-nautical-mile territorial zone).
   - The waters off **Fujairah / Gulf of Oman**.
   - The **Riau Archipelago and Johor anchorage** east of Singapore.
3. **Crude Blending & Origin Washing:**
   Sanctioned crude is blended with non-sanctioned crudes (e.g., blending Urals with Kazakh or Malaysian crude) until its chemical fingerprint changes, issued fresh bills of lading labeled as "Malaysian Blend" or "Singstat residual oil," and sold to independent Chinese private refiners ("teapots") or Indian state refiners.
4. **Non-Western Insurance (P&I Clubs):**
   Prior to 2022, 90% of global maritime insurance was underwritten by the International Group of P&I Clubs in London and Europe. Shadow fleet vessels utilize Russian state-backed reinsurance (RNRC) or domestic non-Western insurers that do not comply with the G7 $60/barrel price cap mechanism.

## 3. The Enforcement Dilemma for Washington
- Physical interdiction by the U.S. Navy or Coast Guard is legally fraught in international waters under the UN Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS) without a UN Security Council mandate (which Russia and China would veto).
- Boarding and seizing foreign-flagged vessels risks immediate military escalation (as seen when Iran seized commercial tankers in the Strait of Hormuz in retaliation for U.S. seizures of Iranian oil off Texas).
- As a result, the parallel trade infrastructure operates with virtual impunity, moving **over 4 to 5 million barrels per day** outside the dollar clearing system.
