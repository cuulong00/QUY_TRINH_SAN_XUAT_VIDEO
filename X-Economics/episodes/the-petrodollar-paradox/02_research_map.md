   - *Argument:* The decline in reserve share (to 57%) reflects portfolio diversification into non-traditional currencies (CAD, AUD) and gold, not an imminent displacement by China's Renminbi (which remains trapped at ~2.3% due to capital controls).

## 4. Five Systemic Macro Mechanisms (Mandatory)

1. **Mechanism 1 — Petrodollar Recycling Loop:** Oil billed in USD $\rightarrow$ Importers buy USD $\rightarrow$ Exporters accumulate dollar surplus $\rightarrow$ Surplus reinvested in US Treasuries $\rightarrow$ U.S. deficit funded at lower interest rates.
2. **Mechanism 2 — Safe Asset Collateral Premium:** Global banking systems require US Treasuries as pristine collateral for repo liquidity; scarcity of non-US safe debt creates structural baseline demand.
3. **Mechanism 3 — Financial Weapon Diminishing Returns:** Freezing sovereign reserves achieves immediate tactical shock, but destroys the psychological neutrality of the currency, accelerating long-term reserve diversification.
4. **Mechanism 4 — Chokepoint Asymmetry:** 82% of Hormuz oil and 75% of Malacca crude flows to Asia. A single naval carrier strike group at these straits commands disproportionate economic leverage over industrial consumers.
5. **Mechanism 5 — The Kindleberger Hegemonic Trap:** Providing the global public good of maritime security incurs astronomical domestic costs for the hegemon while subsidizing the free-riding economic growth of competitor nations.
