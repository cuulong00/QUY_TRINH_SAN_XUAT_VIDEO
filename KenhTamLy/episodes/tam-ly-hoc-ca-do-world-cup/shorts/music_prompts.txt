[Track 1 - Dopamine Short Soundtrack]
Genre & Style: cinematic dark ambient electronic, hybrid modular synth score, transition to contemplative acoustic ambient
Mood & Emotion: starts with cold documentary tension and psychological unease, transitions to an overwhelming sense of internal craving, followed by a sudden dramatic drop into cold realization, finally resolving to warm peaceful clarity
Instrumentation: low muted cello pulse, syncopated analog synthesizer plucks, dry ticking clock sound effects, deep sub-bass hum, resolving to sparse organic acoustic guitar notes and a single resonant Tibetan singing bowl ring
Tempo & Rhythm: starts at 90 BPM with a tense heartbeat-like driving pulse, accelerates slightly with rapid syncopated clicks, drops into near-silence at 45 seconds with only a low drone, resolving to a slow 60 BPM atmospheric warmth for the outro
Vocal Style & Language: instrumental, purely wordless, strictly no vocals, designed with carved-out mid frequencies to preserve space for a low male voiceover
