## Section 2: Philippine Conglomerates & Oligopoly
- **Conglomerate Dominance:** The Philippine economy is heavily dominated by a few giant family-owned conglomerates, primarily SM Investments Corporation (SMIC), Ayala Corporation, and San Miguel Corporation (SMC).
- **San Miguel Corporation (SMC) Revenue:** SMC's revenues are equivalent to approximately 6% to 6.8% of the country's total GDP. It dominates energy, tollways, food & beverage, and packaging.
- **SM Investments Corp (SMIC):** Dominates retail, malls (SM Prime is the largest), and banking (BDO Unibank).
- **Oligopoly Impact:** These conglomerates dominate cash-generative service and retail sectors. They are slow and risk-averse regarding high-tech manufacturing, preventing small and medium enterprises (SMEs) from integrating and moving up the technology value chain. Consequently, the middle class in the Philippines is shrinking.

## Section 4: Public Transit Modernization Comparison
- **Philippines PTMP (PUVMP):** The Public Transport Modernization Program was launched in 2017 to phase out traditional jeepneys and replace them with Euro-4/electric mini-buses.
- **PTMP Delays in 2026:** In mid-2026, the program is deemed "not viable in its present state" due to heavy protests from drivers, high vehicle costs, and cooperative debt burdens. Malacañang has announced there is no immediate mandatory phase-out.
- **Vietnam Public Transport:** Vietnam has rapidly introduced modern electric buses (VinBus) with digital displays and air conditioning, manufactured by a domestic firm (VinFast).
