> The Persistence of US Dollar Hegemony Despite these developments, the US Dollar's dominance in the global financial system remains highly resilient [cite: 6]. The Bank for International Settlements (BIS) Triennial Central Bank Survey, conducted in April 2025, confirmed that the US dollar was on one side of approximately 89% of all foreign exchange transactions, unchanged from previous periods [cite: 6]. Furthermore, IMF data from late 2025 showed that the US dollar constituted over 56% to 57% of global official foreign exchange reserves, while the Renminbi's share remained steady at approximately 2% [cite: 6, 51].
