# Research Vault — Note 09: SWIFT, Financial Cleared Settlement & Alternative Rails

## Metadata & Executive Summary
- **Topic:** SWIFT Market Share, CHIPS Clearing Network, and the Operational Reality of Alternative Payment Systems (CIPS, SPFS, mBridge, BRICS Pay).
- **Source Agencies:** SWIFT RMB Tracker, Clearing House Interbank Payments System (CHIPS), People's Bank of China (PBOC), Bank of Russia, Atlantic Council GeoEconomics Center.
- **Core Mechanism:** The Network Effect Moat — While alternative cross-border financial messaging systems have expanded, they remain severely constrained by lack of foreign-exchange liquidity, absence of continuous linked settlement, and counterparty credit risks.

---

## 1. Verified Financial Rails Statistics (2025/2026)
- **SWIFT Network Dominance:**
  - Connects over **11,500 financial institutions** in over **200 countries and territories**.
  - Handles over **45 million financial messaging transactions per day**.
  - **Currency Breakdown of SWIFT Customer-Initiated & Institutional Payments:**
    * **U.S. Dollar (USD):** **46.5% – 48.2%** of all global SWIFT payment messages.
    * **Euro (EUR):** **22.5% – 24.1%**.
    * **British Pound (GBP):** **6.8% – 7.2%**.
    * **Japanese Yen (JPY):** **3.8% – 4.2%**.
    * **Chinese Renminbi (RMB):** **3.9% – 4.7%** (up from 1.9% in 2021).

## 2. Alternative Settlement Rails (The Contenders)
1. **CIPS (Cross-Border Interbank Payment System - China):**
   - Processed approximately **120+ trillion RMB ($17 trillion USD)** annually by 2025.
   - **The Structural Flaw:** CIPS is primarily a settlement system, but for cross-border messaging to foreign banks without direct lines, **~80% of CIPS transactions still rely on SWIFT messaging standards** to communicate instructions.
2. **SPFS (System for Transfer of Financial Messages - Russia):**
   - Created in 2014 following Crimea sanctions.
   - Used extensively within Russia, Belarus, and limited Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU) partners.
   - Suffers from near-zero Western/Global bank connectivity, lack of 24/7 liquidity clearing, and severe risk of secondary OFAC sanctions on any foreign bank connecting to it.
3. **Project mBridge (Multi-CBDC Cross-Border Platform):**
   - Joint initiative by the BIS Innovation Hub, PBOC (China), HKMA (Hong Kong), Bank of Thailand, and Central Bank of the UAE (Saudi Arabia joined in June 2024).
   - Utilizes distributed ledger technology (DLT) for real-time, peer-to-peer foreign exchange settlement without routing through Western correspondent banks.
   - The most technically viable long-term threat to dollar clearing, but currently handles under $10 billion in test transactions—orders of magnitude below the $7.5 trillion daily global FX market.

## 3. The Core Dilemma: Bilateral Currency Accumulation Trap
When countries attempt to bypass the dollar via bilateral local currencies, they hit the **Non-Convertibility Wall**:
- **The Russian Rupee Trap (2022–2023):** Russia sold tens of billions of dollars worth of discounted crude to India settled in Indian Rupees. Because India runs a structural trade deficit with Russia and the Rupee is non-convertible, Russian oil companies accumulated an estimated **$30–$40 billion in stranded Rupees** in Indian bank accounts that they could not repatriate or convert into goods they needed.
- This economic reality forces commodity exporters back towards either the U.S. Dollar, physical Gold, or direct barter.
