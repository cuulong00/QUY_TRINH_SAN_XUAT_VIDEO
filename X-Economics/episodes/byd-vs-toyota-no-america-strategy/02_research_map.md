## 3. Counter-Thesis Candidates (Mandatory Multi-Perspective Steelman)

1. **Counter-Thesis 1 (The Empty-Calorie Volume Trap):**
   - *Steelman Argument:* BYD may surpass Toyota in unit volume by selling millions of sub-$15,000 cars in developing markets, but it will remain a financial dwarf. Toyota’s $34.5B operating profit gives it the balance sheet fortress to absorb multi-billion-dollar R&D cycles, solid-state battery commercialization, and massive recalls that would crush a thinner-margin player like BYD.
   - *Data Point:* Toyota profit per vehicle is ~$3,400 vs. BYD domestic profit of ~$500-$1,150.

2. **Counter-Thesis 2 (The 60-Year Moat of Global South Residual Value):**
   - *Steelman Argument:* A Toyota Hilux or Land Cruiser in Africa, Latin America, or the Middle East is not just transport; it is a financial asset (retains 70-80% value after 3-5 years) with bulletproof durability. In contrast, Chinese EVs face rapid software obsolescence, battery degradation, lack of mechanics, and steep second-hand depreciation.
   - *Data Point:* Over 1 billion people live in regions with frequent blackouts or no charging infrastructure, making self-charging hybrids the only practical electrification path.

3. **Counter-Thesis 3 (The Tariff Contagion Wall):**
   - *Steelman Argument:* BYD assumes the rest of the world will remain an open playground while America stays walled off. In reality, Brazil (35%), Turkey (40%), EU (27%), and India (blocking FDI) prove that governments will protect domestic jobs and local tax bases from Chinese industrial dumping.
   - *Data Point:* Turkey suspended BYD’s $1B factory incentives, and Mexico bowed to USMCA pressure by freezing Chinese automaker state subsidies.
