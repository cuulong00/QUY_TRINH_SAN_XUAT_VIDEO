# Research Vault — Note 06: NDS 2022 & The Doctrine of Integrated Deterrence

## Metadata & Executive Summary
- **Topic:** The 2022 U.S. National Defense Strategy (NDS), The Doctrine of Integrated Deterrence, and the Merging of Financial and Naval Power.
- **Source Agencies:** U.S. Department of Defense (DoD NDS 2022), Congressional Research Service (CRS Report R47474), RAND Corporation.
- **Core Mechanism:** Cross-Domain Compellence — How the Pentagon formally codified the synchronization of military, economic, technological, and diplomatic instruments to deter strategic adversaries.

---

## 1. Core Architecture of the 2022 National Defense Strategy
Released in October 2022 by Defense Secretary Lloyd Austin, the NDS established a hierarchy of strategic threats:
1. **The Pacing Challenge:** The People's Republic of China (PRC) is designated as the only competitor with both the intent and the growing economic, diplomatic, military, and technological power to reshape the international order.
2. **The Acute Threat:** The Russian Federation is classified as an immediate, destructive, and revanchist threat to Euro-Atlantic stability.
3. **Persistent Threats:** The Islamic Republic of Iran and North Korea (DPRK) are designated as persistent regional disruptors.

---

## 2. Defining "Integrated Deterrence"
Unlike Cold War containment which relied predominantly on nuclear triad balance and conventional forward presence, **Integrated Deterrence** mandates the seamless integration of:
- **Across Domains:** Air, land, sea, space, cyberspace, and the electromagnetic spectrum.
- **Across Instruments of National Power:** Military, intelligence, export controls (BIS entity lists), financial sanctions (Treasury OFAC), investment screening (CFIUS), and diplomacy.
- **Across Allies and Partners:** Seamless coordination with NATO, AUKUS, Quad, and bilateral treaty allies (Japan, South Korea, Philippines, Australia).

---

## 3. The Financial-Military Feedback Loop
1. **Financial Hegemony as the First Line of Defense:**
   The ability of the U.S. Treasury to freeze sovereign assets, sever access to the Clearing House Interbank Payments System (CHIPS), and threaten secondary sanctions on foreign commercial banks serves as non-kinetic artillery.
2. **The Erosion Trap (Why Hard Naval Power Must Step In):**
   - As documented in the 2022–2026 economic campaigns, the more aggressively the financial weapon is wielded, the faster adversaries build sovereign firewalls (local currency swaps, gold accumulation, domestic payment switches).
   - Once financial sanctions reach their point of diminishing returns, the doctrine requires physical instruments to enforce economic blockades: naval interdiction of sanctioned tankers, coastal monitoring, and maritime chokepoint control.
   - Therefore, U.S. naval deployments in the Persian Gulf and Western Pacific are the ultimate backstop when digital financial sanctions are circumvented.
