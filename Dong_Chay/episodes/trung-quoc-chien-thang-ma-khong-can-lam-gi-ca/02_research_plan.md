## 2. Prompt Nạp Nguồn Cấu Trúc (Structured Ingestion Prompt Design)

```text
Comprehensive geopolitical, macroeconomic, and strategic defense research on how China strategically benefits and insulates itself from the US-Iran Middle East conflict and Straits of Hormuz disruption, with a special deep dive into US Ally Psychology and Geopolitical Realignment:

1. ALLY PSYCHOLOGY & SECURITY ANXIETIES (CRITICAL FOCUS):
- Taiwan: Psychological impact of US military overstretch in Middle East, ammunition depletion, deterrence credibility in Taiwan Strait, growing anxieties in Taipei regarding Washington's willingness/ability to fight a major war with China.
- East Asian Allies (South Korea & Japan): US redeployment of Patriot missile batteries and carrier strike groups (USS Abraham Lincoln) from Indo-Pacific/Korea to Middle East; vulnerability to Middle East oil disruption (Japan 93%, Korea 70% Hormuz dependency).
- European Allies (EU/NATO): Public and governmental frustration over war-induced energy inflation, spikes in LNG/oil prices, resistance to US pressure for military involvement.
- Gulf States (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar): Infrastructure vulnerability, balancing relations between Washington and Beijing, willingness to accept Petroyuan and Chinese diplomatic mediation.
- Global Public Opinion (Pew Research Center 2026/2025 data): Survey of 36 countries showing 25 favoring China over US (including Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Canada, Greece, Australia).

2. ENERGY FORTRESS & SHOCK ABSORPTION:
- China's Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR) vs US SPR (China >100 days total consumption capacity via gov + SOEs vs US 20 days in 2026).
- China's 20-year mitigation of the "Malacca Dilemma": Coal dominance (60% power, 56% energy mix), Renewable energy & nuclear (22% power generation), domestic energy independence shielding the power grid from oil/gas shocks.
- Demand destruction via Electric Vehicles (EVs): 11% EV fleet share in China vs 3% US, EV adoption as energy security shield.
- Global Clean Tech Export Surge: Chinese market dominance in solar panels (80%), lithium-ion batteries (89%), and EVs (70%); export surge (+60% solar, +110% EV) to Global South amid oil shock.

3. FINANCIAL INFRASTRUCTURE & DE-DOLLARIZATION:
- Cross-Border Interbank Payment System (CIPS): Transaction volume surge to 790B RMB/day (~$115B/day), 15% growth post-conflict; CIPS vs SWIFT dynamics.
- Petroyuan & Bilateral Settlement: Iranian oil discounting and RMB settlement, Russia-China trade >90% non-USD, Iranian transit tolls collected in RMB/Crypto.
- Erosion of US sanctions enforcement power and weaponized dollar mechanisms.

4. HISTORICAL ANALOGS & STRATEGIC MAXIMS:
- Napoleon's maxim: "Never interrupt your enemy when he is making a mistake."
- Comparison of US strategic exhaustion across 3 Middle East interventions (Afghanistan, Iraq, Iran) in 25 years vs China's patient accumulation of productive, industrial, and financial leverage.
```
